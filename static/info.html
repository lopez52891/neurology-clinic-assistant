Medical questions?
That’s out of this demo’s wheelhouse. For symptoms, diagnoses, treatments, tests, or surgeries, please call 1-833-UT-CARES (1-833-882-2737).
Key facts (for quick reference):
  • Address: Health Transformation Building, 1601 Trinity St, Building A, Austin, TX 78712
  • Parking: Attached “Health Center Garage” on Google; not free. Rates: UT Austin Garage Daily Rates
  • Entrances/Elevators: Main entrance at 17th & Trinity, or use parking garage elevators to the 1st floor
  • Finding the clinic: First floor, across from the H-E-B Pharmacy; from the street entrance, pass the front desk and it’ll be on your left
  • Wheelchair/assistance: Call ahead to request assistance; availability may vary
  • Phone: 1-833-UT-CARES (1-833-882-2737)
  • Clinic FAQ: uthealthaustin.org/clinics/ophthalmology/faqs
Scheduling basics:
  • Follow-ups (established patients): Self-schedule via your UTHA account/patient portal.
  • New patients: Call 1-833-UT-CARES to register and schedule; online request available on the clinic site.
  • Arrival time: Plan to arrive 15 minutes early for parking and check-in.
  • Typical visit length: 1–2 hours depending on testing and possible dilation (teaching clinic model).
  • Referrals: Not needed for cataract evaluation or a general eye exam; some plans/conditions may require one — call if you need help.

¿Preguntas médicas?
Están fuera del alcance de este demo. Para síntomas, diagnósticos, tratamientos, pruebas o cirugías, llama al 1-833-UT-CARES (1-833-882-2737).
Datos clave:
  • Dirección: Health Transformation Building, 1601 Trinity St, Building A, Austin, TX 78712
  • Estacionamiento: Garaje adjunto “Health Center Garage”; no es gratis. Tarifas: UT Austin Garage Daily Rates
  • Entradas/Ascensores: Entrada principal en 17th & Trinity, o ascensores del garaje al primer piso
